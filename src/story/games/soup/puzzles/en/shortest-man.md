## title
The Shortest Man

## surface
A circus advertises "the shortest man in the world." One day he notices his walking stick has become a little shorter. That night he hangs himself.

## truth
Being the shortest man alive was his living and all he had. The circus's second-shortest man wanted his place and secretly sawed a piece off his stick. Seeing it shorter, he believed he had grown, that he'd lose his title and his job, and in despair he killed himself.

## key_points
- He thought the stick was shorter because he had grown
- His living depended on being the shortest, so growing meant losing everything
- In fact a rival who wanted his place had cut the stick down
