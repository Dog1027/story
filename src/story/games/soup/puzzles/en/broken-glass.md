## title
Two on the Floor

## surface
Fang comes home from work and finds Pearl and Goldie lying dead on the living room floor. There is a puddle of water and broken glass, and the window is open. Nothing in the house is missing.

## truth
Pearl and Goldie were Fang's two goldfish. The cat jumped onto the cabinet and knocked the fishbowl off; it smashed, the water spilled, and the fish died out of the water. The window was open because that's how the cat comes and goes.

## key_points
- Pearl and Goldie were goldfish
- Their bowl broke and they died out of water
- The cat knocked the bowl over
