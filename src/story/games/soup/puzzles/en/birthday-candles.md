## title
Birthday Candles

## surface
Lily makes a wish over her cake and blows out the candles. Every adult in the room starts crying, and Lily beams with joy.

## truth
Lily was born blind. This year she had a corneal transplant, and this is the first time she has ever seen candlelight. Her family had waited years for this moment; the adults are crying because they are so happy.

## key_points
- Lily was blind
- After surgery, this is the first time she sees candlelight
- The adults are crying with joy
