## title
The Dead Number

## surface
Every night before bed, Jake dials the same number. All he ever hears is "The number you have dialed is not in service." He always listens to the end before hanging up, and then he can sleep.

## truth
Jake's mother used to record voice prompts for the phone company, and that "not in service" message is her voice. She has since died, but the recording is still in use. Jake misses her, and he dials a dead number every night just to hear her voice again.

## key_points
- The recording is his mother's voice
- His mother has died
- He calls to hear his mother's voice
