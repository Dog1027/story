## title
The Stranger at the Funeral

## surface
At her mother's funeral, Ling notices a man she has never seen before and falls for him at first sight, but she never gets his name or any way to reach him. A few days later, Ling kills her own sister.

## truth
Ling wanted to see the man again. She was sure he knew the family, so if there were another funeral in the family he would probably come. She killed her sister so there would be another funeral.

## key_points
- She wanted to see the man from the funeral again
- She believed he would come to another family funeral
- She killed her sister to cause another funeral
