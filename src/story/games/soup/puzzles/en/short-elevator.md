## title
The Seventh Floor

## surface
Wei lives on the tenth floor of an apartment building. Every morning he takes the elevator down to the ground floor. Coming home, though, he always rides only to the seventh floor and walks up the last three. Only on rainy days, or when someone else is in the elevator, does he ride all the way to ten.

## truth
Wei is very short: standing in the elevator, the highest button he can reach is seven. On rainy days he carries an umbrella and presses ten with its tip; when someone else is in the elevator, he asks them to press it for him.

## key_points
- Wei is too short to reach the button for the tenth floor
- On rainy days he presses it with his umbrella, and otherwise asks someone else to press it
