## title
The Tunnel

## surface
A man sits on a train, smiling as he watches the scenery go by. The train enters a long tunnel and the carriage goes pitch black. By the time the train comes out, he has jumped to his death.

## truth
The man had been blind from birth and had just had surgery that restored his sight; this was his first look at the world. When the train went into the tunnel and everything went black, he believed the surgery had failed and he was blind again, and in that moment of despair he jumped from the train.

## key_points
- He had been blind and had just had surgery to see
- In the dark tunnel he thought he had gone blind again
- He jumped because he believed the surgery had failed
