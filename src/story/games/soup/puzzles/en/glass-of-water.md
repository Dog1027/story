## title
A Glass of Water

## surface
A man walks into a bar and asks the bartender for a glass of water. The bartender looks at him and suddenly shouts in his face. The man jumps, then smiles, thanks him, and leaves without drinking anything.

## truth
The man had hiccups he couldn't stop and wanted water to get rid of them. The bartender saw it and shouted to give him a fright. The scare stopped the hiccups, so he no longer needed the water and left happily.

## key_points
- The man had hiccups
- He wanted the water to cure them
- The bartender scared him on purpose, which stopped the hiccups
