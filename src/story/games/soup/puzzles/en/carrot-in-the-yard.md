## title
A Carrot in the Yard

## surface
One March morning, a woman finds a carrot, two small black stones and a red scarf lying together on the grass in her yard. Nobody came into the yard last night.

## truth
They were from a snowman the children built in the yard during winter: the carrot was its nose, the stones its eyes, the scarf around its neck. The weather warmed up and the snowman melted overnight, leaving only these behind on the grass.

## key_points
- The things were parts of a snowman
- It got warmer and the snowman melted
