## title
After You

## surface
A man waits a long time in line at the bank, but when his turn comes he lets the person behind him go first. He keeps letting people go ahead, a dozen of them, until police officers rush in.

## truth
When his turn came, he noticed the teller was pale: a robber with a knife was hiding behind the counter, forcing her to fill a bag with money. Without showing it, he kept stepping aside and pretending to look for something while secretly calling the police on his phone, stalling until they arrived.

## key_points
- The bank was being robbed
- The man noticed
- He let others go first to stall until the police he called arrived
