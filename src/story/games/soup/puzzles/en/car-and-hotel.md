## title
The Man Who Pushed His Car

## surface
A man pushes his car along until it stops in front of a hotel. The moment he sees the hotel, he knows he is bankrupt.

## truth
He was playing Monopoly with friends. His token was the little car; he moved it forward and it landed on a square where a friend had built a hotel. He couldn't pay the rent, so he went bankrupt in the game.

## key_points
- He was playing Monopoly
- His token, the car, landed on a square with someone else's hotel
- He couldn't pay the rent and went bankrupt in the game
