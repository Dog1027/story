## title
Turtle Soup

## surface
A man walks into a seaside restaurant and orders turtle soup. After one sip he asks the owner, "Is this really turtle soup?" The owner says it is. The man pays, walks out, and kills himself that night.

## truth
Years ago the man and his wife were shipwrecked on a desert island. His wife died there, and the other survivors, afraid he wouldn't make it, fed him a "turtle soup" so he lived until rescue. Today he tasted real turtle soup and it tasted nothing like it: he realized the soup on the island was made from his wife's flesh, and couldn't bear it.

## key_points
- He had once been shipwrecked on an island
- The "turtle soup" he ate there was made from his dead wife
- Real turtle soup tasted different, so he realized the truth
