## title
The Unopened Pack

## surface
In the middle of an empty field, a man lies dead with an unopened pack beside him. There are no footprints nearby and no tracks from any vehicle.

## truth
The "pack" was his parachute. He jumped from a plane, the parachute failed to open, and he fell to his death in the middle of the field, which is why there are no footprints.

## key_points
- The pack was a parachute that didn't open
- He jumped from a plane and died in the fall
