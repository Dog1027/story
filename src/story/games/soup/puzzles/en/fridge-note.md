## title
The Note on the Fridge

## surface
Every morning, Ken's roommate sticks a note on the fridge: "Don't eat my pudding." One morning there is no note, and Ken immediately calls an ambulance.

## truth
The roommate has a serious heart condition and could have an attack in his sleep at any time. They agreed that he would put a note on the fridge every morning to show he was fine. No note meant he might not have woken up, so Ken called for help right away.

## key_points
- The note is an agreed sign that the roommate is okay
- The roommate has a condition that could strike at any time
- No note means he may be in trouble
