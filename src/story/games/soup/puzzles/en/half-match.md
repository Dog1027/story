## title
Half a Match

## surface
In the middle of the desert lies the body of a naked man, holding half a match in his fist. There are no footprints anywhere around him.

## truth
He and some friends were crossing the desert in a hot-air balloon when it started losing height. They threw out everything, even their clothes, and it was still too heavy, so they drew matches to decide who would jump: whoever drew the short one. He drew the broken match, jumped, and died from the fall, so there were no footprints.

## key_points
- He jumped from a hot-air balloon
- They had thrown out everything, even their clothes, to lose weight
- They drew matches to choose who jumped, and he drew the broken one
