## title
The Sunny-Day Umbrella

## surface
Mrs. Wang always carries an open umbrella when she goes out on a sunny day. On rainy days she never uses one.

## truth
Mrs. Wang has a condition that makes her severely allergic to sunlight; her skin blisters in the sun, so on sunny days she shades herself with the umbrella. On rainy days there is no sun to block, and she loves walking in the rain.

## key_points
- The umbrella is for blocking the sun, not the rain
- She is severely allergic to sunlight
