## title
The Silent Listener

## surface
When the concert ends, the whole hall gives a standing ovation, except an old man in the front row who never claps. After her bow, the pianist steps down from the stage and bows deeply to him.

## truth
The old man is the pianist's father, and he is completely deaf. Throughout the concert he kept both hands pressed against the wooden edge of the stage, feeling the vibrations of the music, so he never clapped. She chose pieces with heavy bass so that her father could "hear" her concert this way.

## key_points
- The old man is deaf
- He is the pianist's father
- He listened by feeling the vibrations through the stage
