## title
The Dark Lighthouse

## surface
On a stormy night, the lighthouse keeper switches off the light. The next day, every newspaper in the country calls him a hero.

## truth
It was wartime, and an enemy fleet was trying to sneak into the harbor at night, using the lighthouse to find the channel. The keeper realized it and switched off the light; the ships lost their way in the storm, hit the rocks, and the attack failed.

## key_points
- It was wartime
- Enemy ships were navigating by the lighthouse
- Without the light they lost their way and the attack failed
