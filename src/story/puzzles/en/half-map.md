## title
Half a Map

## surface
A hiker tears his map in half in a mountain hut, leaves one half on the table, and sets off. Three days later, that half of the map saves his life.

## truth
He was hiking alone and worried that no one would know where he had gone if something happened. He drew his planned route on the map and left the half showing it in the hut. On the way he fell and was stranded; rescuers found the half map in the hut and followed the route to find him.

## key_points
- The half he left shows his planned route
- He got hurt and stranded on the mountain
- Rescuers used that half map to find him
